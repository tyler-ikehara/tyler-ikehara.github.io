The FR3 band combines extreme bandwidth with physically large antenna arrays. In this regime, conventional phase-shift beamforming suffers from beam squint and has to be replaced by true time delay (TTD) beamforming. This invited paper shows that TTD beamforming is mathematically equivalent to applying the Radon transform to space/time measurements. Using results from computed tomography and seismic exploration, we develop methods to remove far-field signals, estimate near-field angles of arrival by triangulation, and extract individual signal envelopes by integrating along hyperbolic trajectories.
